05 / How an episode is built

Real research.
Honest about the rest.

Our host and voices are AI-generated. The research, facts and sources are real, and listed in every video description. We'd rather tell you than have you wonder.

  1. 01

    Pick the turn

    A car with a near-failure, a ban, a rescue or a comeback. Never a repeat.

  2. 02

    Build the reference sheet

    Engine code, output by market, key dates and people. Every line gets a source; anything unsourced is cut or softened.

  3. 03

    Script and footage

    Real archive footage first. Licences logged, credits in the description.

  4. 04

    Check before upload

    Facts against sources, pronunciation, sync, sound. Then it ships.

06 / Questions

Frequently asked

What is Piston Origins?

Piston Origins is a car-history channel on YouTube. Each full episode tells the origin story of one legendary car, such as the Toyota Supra Mk4, Nissan Skyline GT-R R34, Mazda RX-7, Honda NSX or Toyota AE86, and each 60-second Short reveals one little-known fact about a car or engine.

Who presents Piston Origins? Is the host real?

The host, Skye, and the narration voice are AI-generated. The research, facts and sources are real, and every source is listed in the video description and on the episode page on this site.

How often does Piston Origins post new videos?

A new full car story every week, plus 60-second car secrets in YouTube Shorts.

Where do Piston Origins' facts come from?

Each episode is written from a reference sheet in which every fact has a source: manufacturer press material, magazines such as Car and Driver, Top Gear and Hagerty, auction results and reference works. Those sources are listed on each episode page. Anything that cannot be sourced is cut or softened.

Which cars has Piston Origins covered?

Toyota Supra Mk4, Nissan Skyline GT-R R34, Mazda RX-7 and 787B, Toyota AE86, Honda NSX, Lexus LFA, Nissan GT-R R35, Toyota Celica GT-Four ST205, and the Ferrari vs Lamborghini rivalry.

Can I suggest a car for Piston Origins to cover?

Yes. Leave a comment on any Piston Origins video on YouTube or send a message on Instagram (@pistonorigins). Topics are picked from the comments.
