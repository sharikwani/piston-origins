05 / How an episode is built

Real research.
Honest about the rest.

Our host and voices are AI-generated. The research, facts and sources are real, and listed in every video description. We'd rather tell you than have you wonder.

  1. 01

    Pick the turn

    A car with a near-failure, a ban, a rescue or a comeback. Never a repeat.

  2. 02

    Build the reference sheet

    Engine code, output by market, key dates and people. Every line gets a source; anything unsourced is cut or softened.

  3. 03

    Script and footage

    Real archive footage first. Licences logged, credits in the description.

  4. 04

    Check before upload

    Facts against sources, pronunciation, sync, sound. Then it ships.
