EP 09 · Rally · Toyota Celica GT-Four ST205

Toyota Cheated So Well, the FIA Called It Genius

27 October 2026 · 5:43 · 38 views on YouTube

How Toyota Team Europe hid an illegal turbo restrictor in the 1995 World Rally Championship, in the Celica GT-Four ST205, and why FIA president Max Mosley called it genius. Toyota was leading both world championships with two rallies left. Then scrutineers at Rally Catalunya took Auriol's car apart.

Quick answers

Why was Toyota banned from the 1995 World Rally Championship?

At Rally Catalunya in October 1995, scrutineers took apart Didier Auriol's Toyota Celica GT-Four ST205 and found that Toyota Team Europe had engineered a way around the mandatory turbo restrictor, letting the engine breathe more air than the rules allowed. Toyota was excluded from the 1995 championship.

What did the FIA say about Toyota's turbo cheat?

FIA president Max Mosley famously praised its ingenuity, calling it the most ingenious thing he had seen in motorsport, even as the team was punished.
