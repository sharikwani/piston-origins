EP 01 · Origin · Toyota Supra Mk4 (A80)

The Toyota Supra Mk4: How Toyota Built a Legend

4 October 2026 · 3:34 · 320 views on YouTube

How did a Japanese sports coupe from 1993 become one of the most wanted cars on the planet? From the 1967 Toyota 2000GT to the first Celica Supra, chief engineer Isao Tsuzuki's "less is more" Mk4, and the legendary 2JZ-GTE with its sequential twin turbos, this is the full story of the A80 Toyota Supra.

Quick answers

What engine is in the Mk4 Toyota Supra?

The Mk4 (A80) Supra Turbo uses the 2JZ-GTE, a 3.0-litre inline-six with sequential twin turbochargers, famous for handling huge power with modest upgrades.

Who designed the Mk4 Toyota Supra?

Chief engineer Isao Tsuzuki led the A80 Supra with a "less is more" philosophy, cutting weight wherever possible.

Why is the Mk4 Supra so expensive?

Its tuning potential, the 2JZ-GTE engine and its role in Hollywood and tuner culture made the A80 Supra one of the most wanted Japanese cars. One sold for $550,000.
