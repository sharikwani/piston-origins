EP 08 · Rivalry · Lamborghini 350 GT and Miura

Ferrari vs Lamborghini: The Insult That Started It All

24 October 2026 · 7:44 · 322 views on YouTube

Ferruccio Lamborghini was a tractor maker who drove Ferraris. When the clutch kept failing, he went to Maranello to complain, and Enzo Ferrari supposedly told him he'd never handle a Ferrari. This is the full Lamborghini vs Ferrari origin story.

He built a V12 rival in four months, showed a car with no engine, then shocked the world with the Miura, the car that invented the supercar. Was the insult real, or a story Ferruccio liked to tell? And who actually won: Ferrari or Lamborghini?

Quick answers

Why did Ferruccio Lamborghini start making cars?

Ferruccio Lamborghini was a tractor maker who owned Ferraris. When the clutch kept failing he complained to Enzo Ferrari, who, as Ferruccio told the story, said a tractor maker could never handle a Ferrari. Ferruccio decided to build his own grand tourer. His biographer argues the real motive was economic: there was money in high-end cars.

Was Lamborghini's first car really shown without an engine?

Yes. The Lamborghini 350 GTV was displayed at the 1963 Turin Motor Show without an engine. The production 350 GT followed at Geneva in 1964, and 120 were built.

Who sells more cars today, Ferrari or Lamborghini?

In 2025 Lamborghini delivered 10,747 cars and Ferrari shipped 13,640.
