EP 05 · Origin · Honda NSX (NA1)

Why Ferrari FEARED This Honda | Honda NSX Story

14 October 2026 · 2:44 · 324 views on YouTube

The full Honda NSX (Acura NSX) history: how Ayrton Senna called Honda's secret supercar "fragile" at Suzuka in 1989, and how Honda answered with an aluminium body, a VTEC V6 and a chassis 50% stiffer, the car that woke up Ferrari.

From the 1984 Pininfarina HP-X concept and the NS-X reveal at the 1989 Chicago Auto Show, to Senna's test, eight more months of Nürburgring development with Motoharu Kurosawa, the 3.0-litre C30A V6 with titanium connecting rods revving to 8,000 rpm, the hand-built cars from Tochigi, and what a $60,000 Acura NSX is worth today, this is the full NSX story.

Quick answers

What did Ayrton Senna say about the Honda NSX?

Testing a prototype at Suzuka in 1989, Ayrton Senna called the NSX "fragile". Honda spent about eight more months developing the car, including at the Nürburgring, and made the chassis around 50% stiffer.

What engine does the original Honda NSX have?

The C30A, a 3.0-litre V6 with VTEC and titanium connecting rods that revs to 8,000 rpm.

How much is an original Honda NSX worth?

The Acura NSX cost about $60,000 new. Good examples now trade around $80,000 to $120,000, and one sold at auction for $155,911.
